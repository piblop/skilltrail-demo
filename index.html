The problem we're solving

Fresh graduate. Zero years' experience. Every "entry-level" ad wants two.

Grad roles are shrinking. Applications per role are at record highs. AI is eating the junior tasks grads used to learn on. You can't compete on work history you don't have.

01 · The insight

Your transcript is the experience.

SkillTrail finds you a job from your transcript and academics alone. Subjects, marks, capstones — read from the official handbook, turned into a skills profile with proof.

02 · The wedge

Adjacent roles are the way in.

Don't wait for the perfect title. SkillTrail surfaces every role your evidence supports — Aligned, Adjacent, Stretch. Take a nearby role, bank industry experience, work toward the ideal job from inside the market.

03 · The moat

Built for a market AI keeps redrawing.

Matching on evidence, not titles re-maps you to whatever the market becomes. And nothing is fabricated — every resume line traces back to something you did.

Why now

~300,000 new Australian graduates a year. Job boards rank them last on experience. SkillTrail uses the one thing they already own — their academic record. Transcript in, matches and apply kit out.
